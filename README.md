# Cultural Home Bias in the International Art Market
<p align="center">
  <em>Bachelor's Thesis in Finance · Stockholm School of Economics</em>
</p>

This project investigates cultural home bias in international art auction markets as part of my Bachelor's thesis at the Stockholm School of Economics.

Using art auction transaction data, I apply hedonic pricing models and country-pair regressions to examine differences in artwork prices across domestic and foreign markets.
